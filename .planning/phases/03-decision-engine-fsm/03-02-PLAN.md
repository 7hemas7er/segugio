<objective>
Extend the core `DecisionEngine` with the four guards that protect the broadcast: the feed-alive guard + feed-death emergency cut (DEC-06), the action-suppression hold (DEC-03), the expected-occlusion hold (DEC-04), and the deterministic tiebreaker (DEC-05). Every hold carries a mandatory tick max-cap so a stuck signal can never freeze the show.

Purpose: These are the fail-safe and anti-oscillation guards. DEC-06 (never air a dead feed) is the catastrophic-failure guard the whole project's robustness constraint ("nessun crash ammesso", SAFE fallback) hinges on. DEC-03/04 keep the camera through climaxes and tunnels; DEC-05 stops equidistant-score ping-pong the hysteresis dead-band alone does not fully settle.
Output: An engine that excludes dead feeds from candidacy, emergency-cuts off a dying live feed (bypassing dwell/cooldown, then re-protecting the new target), suppresses switching while held (with caps), and resolves ties to the incumbent (or config order). DEC-03/04/05/06 test suites green.
</objective>
