<objective>
Make scoring rules config-driven (SCP-01) with fail-fast validation (SCP-02). Add a pydantic v2 `ScoringProfile` sub-model (ordered zone-band list + bonus list + freestyle-category list + agility model/fault/rate fields) to the CFG-03 discriminated union in `config/discipline.py`, plus a pydantic→stdlib adapter that converts the validated profile into the plain `ScoringProfile` dataclass `score_calc` consumes (D-10 — `score_calc` never imports the config models). Ship three config fixtures: CSEN 2026 (the pinned default), FIDASC (alternate, numbers `[ASSUMED]`, gated behind a human-verify checkpoint), and a generic FCI-style agility example. Prove a CSEN→FIDASC swap changes the result with no code change, and that a malformed profile fails fast at config-load.

Purpose: SCP-01/SCP-02 are the "swap federation without touching code" guarantee. Shipping a REAL second federation (FIDASC) with a genuinely different shape (5 zones vs 4, single +1 vs two +0.5, 60s vs 90s, no best-N) is what proves success-criterion 3 against real alternate rules.
Output: the pydantic scoring sub-model + adapter in `discipline.py`, three YAML profile fixtures, and `tests/test_score_profiles.py` (swap + malformed fail-fast).
</objective>
