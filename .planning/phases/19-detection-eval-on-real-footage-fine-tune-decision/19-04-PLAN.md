<objective>
Deliver DET-03: a NEW sibling `run_detection_eval` in `eval.py` that measures detector
recall AND precision per condition at IoU 0.5 on the FP16 batched forward the production loop
actually ships — NOT the eager FP32 serial `track()` path that `run_clip_eval` runs today
(the exact P04 mistake DET-03 rejects, Pitfall 1), and NOT the `lock_held` proxy.

Purpose: the fine-tune decision (DET-04) must be grounded in the numerics that ship. The
harness reuses `ClipFrameSource` (same stride as the GT), resolves the dog BY NAME, runs
`model.predict(..., end2end=False, half=True, classes=[dog_idx], verbose=False)` (mirror of
`batched.py:128-134`, conf at the shipped default 0.25), and scores RAW detection boxes against
the committed GT via the pure `score_frame`/`parse_gt` from 19-01. Tracker-confirmed recall is
Phase-20 (CAL-02) territory (Open Question 1) — the primary metric is raw detector output.
Output: `run_detection_eval` + `_xywhn_to_corners` in `eval.py`, and a `@pytest.mark.gpu`
harness test that proves the FP16 forward and returns recall/precision.
</objective>
