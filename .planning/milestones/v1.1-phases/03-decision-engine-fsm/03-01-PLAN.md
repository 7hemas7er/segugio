  <behavior>
    - DEC-01: `engine.tick(...)` computes a candidate via a pure `max(...)` over eligible scores but NEVER assigns `self.target` directly; the target changes ONLY inside a `transitions` callback whose `conditions` passed. A trigger whose guard fails is a silent no-op (target unchanged).
    - DEC-02 (hysteresis): a challenger camera must exceed the incumbent's score by `hysteresis_margin` to even become a challenger; it must then sustain that lead for `debounce_N` consecutive ticks (the challenger streak counter) before a cut fires. A challenger that drops below the margin on any tick resets the streak.
    - DEC-02 (dwell): no normal auto-switch fires until `dwell_elapsed >= min_dwell_ticks` on the current target. `dwell_elapsed` resets to 0 on every target change.
    - DEC-02 (cooldown): no normal auto-switch fires while `cooldown_remaining > 0`. `cooldown_remaining` is set to `cooldown_ticks` on every target change and decremented each tick to a floor of 0.
    - DEC-02 (no-chase): replaying `score_stream(ping_pong)` yields ≤ 1 transition after warm-up; replaying `tie` yields ≤ 1. (Count transitions, NOT per-tick argmax — RESEARCH Pitfall 3.)
    - Cold start: the engine begins in `Mode.safe` with `target == "SAFE"` and switches to the first camera that clears the hysteresis upper threshold + sustains `debounce_N`.
    - Fail-safe: a NaN/inf/out-of-range `CameraScore.score` makes that camera an ineligible candidate (never challenger, never target); `tick()` never raises.
  </behavior>
