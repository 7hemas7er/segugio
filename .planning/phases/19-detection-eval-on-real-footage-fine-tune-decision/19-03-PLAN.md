<objective>
Deliver DET-02: a zero-external-dependency, opencv-only bbox labeling tool
(`tools/label_clips.py`, `cv2.selectROI`) that samples clip frames sparsely via
`ClipFrameSource` and writes a committed `<clip>.gt.yaml` ground-truth sidecar, and then a
labeled ground-truth subset over the decisive + precision-guard disc conditions.

Purpose: DET-03 needs a recall/precision reference that is INDEPENDENT of the detector under
test (D-05 rejected auto-labeling). The operator labels only the single in-competition dog
(zone∧motion subject — NOT handler/bystander, D-07); a skipped frame is `bbox: null` (dog
absent) so precision/false-lock is measurable (D-09). Alignment with the eval is guaranteed by
sharing `ClipFrameSource` + stride and keying by emitted tick.
Output: `tools/label_clips.py`, its headless unit test, and `<clip>.gt.yaml` sidecars covering
a few hundred frames total (D-06).
</objective>
