<objective>
Measure the CHEAP rung first (RESEARCH Open Q2 + primary recommendation): eager `half=True` FP16 + ONE batched detection forward per tick, replacing the 4 serial `model.track()` calls — WITHOUT building a TensorRT engine. This path keeps native-feature BoT-SORT ReID intact (an eager FP16 `.pt` still exposes the in-graph Detect head, so `assert_native_reid_engaged` still passes — it sidesteps the engine↔ReID conflict entirely, RESEARCH Pitfall 1). FP16 roughly halves per-forward compute and batching collapses 4 forwards into 1 — the two levers, applied without the engine's complexity. Re-run `tests/test_latency.py -m gpu` after the change: if p95 < 150ms, the phase goal is MET by the cheap rung and the engine build (06-03) becomes unnecessary; if still over budget, the measurement is the explicit gate that authorizes 06-03.

Purpose: RESEARCH is emphatic — try FP16 + batching BEFORE committing to the engine, because it may already clear 150ms AND preserve native ReID. This plan is the measurement that decides whether Wave 2 (the engine) is even needed.
Output: A batched FP16 `_fan_out` path, a #13107 state-isolation guard test, the re-run gpu latency gate, and a recorded p95 that gates 06-03.
</objective>
