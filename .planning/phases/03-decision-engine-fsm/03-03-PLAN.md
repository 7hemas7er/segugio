<objective>
Add the OVERRIDE operational mode to the `DecisionEngine`: an operator can force a specific camera (applied immediately, bypassing dwell/debounce/cooldown), the override persists through a feed-death of the forced camera (showing SAFE/best-live while dead but never airing the dead feed, snapping back on recovery), it ends only on explicit release, and auto-resume after release requires the auto candidate to win the full hysteresis + min_dwell qualification.

Purpose: RBT-03 is the one operator-in-the-loop affordance in an otherwise operator-less system. The subtle correctness lives in two interactions: override-through-feed-death (D-03 — the dead-feed invariant must hold even under a forced camera) and auto-resume that does not re-introduce flicker (D-06 — reuse the normal-switch guard, no special-case snap).
Output: A three-mode FSM (SAFE/AUTO/OVERRIDE) with force/release guarded transitions and the forced-but-dead fallback; the RBT-03 combined-scenario test green.
</objective>
